Innovation Intellect (OPC) Private Limited

Pioneering Industrial Intelligence & Autonomous Operations

Innovation Intellect (I²) is a premier technology and high-tech engineering enterprise behind the Terrestrial Platform suite. Backed by extensive engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) roots spanning decades of plant-floor experience, we architect end-to-end industrial digital transformations from edge instrumentation to private cloud architectures.

Our Core Philosophy: Transforming complex, continuous process telemetry into deterministic, autonomous decisions, predictive maintenance, and closed-loop execution across energy, utilities, and heavy manufacturing.

Enterprise Capabilities

  • Autonomous AI & Digital Twins: Physics-Informed Neural Operators (PINO), Graph-of-Thought RAG engines, and real-time operational simulation.
  • Industrial Automation & Protocols: Native support for OPC UA, Modbus, MQTT, TSN, BACnet, DLMS/COSEM, and legacy PLC integrations.
  • Clean Energy & Smart Infrastructure: Gigawatt-scale solar PV telemetry, automated BMS, closed-loop HVAC optimization, and grid monitoring.
  • Mission-Critical Reliability: Military-grade cybersecurity, single sign-on (SSO), edge-failover resilience, and deterministic telemetry guarantees.
