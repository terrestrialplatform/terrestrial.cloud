Real-World Case Study Outcomes

Operational Use Cases & Field Impact

Actual documented use cases across Quality Assurance (QA), Reduced Manpower, Efficiency, and Asset Productivity.

Efficiency

Efficiency (Mass & Energy Balance Calculations)

Calculates real-time mass and thermal balances across condensers, heat exchangers, and distillation columns, uncovering energy losses. (Terra Aavtar Mass & Energy Balance).

QA

QA (Thermodynamic Bottleneck Detection)

Detects hydraulic and thermal bottlenecks before physical equipment is fabricated, preventing cavitation and vapor-lock. (Terra Aavtar Thermodynamics).

Productivity

Productivity (Pre-Built Controller I/O Bindings)

Pre-mapped I/O tags allow process engineers to deploy validated setpoints straight to plant controllers with zero re-tagging.

Reduced Manpower

Reduced Manpower (Virtual Metering of Unmetered Streams)

First-principles virtual metering calculates flow rates and enthalpies of intermediate streams without costly physical sensor installations.
