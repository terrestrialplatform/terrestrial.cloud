Comparison with Traditional Legal Search / Naive RAG

Traditional Legal Search & Naive RAG Systems TerraCognizance Terrestrial Platform Advantage
Vector chunking fractures sentences and multi-page contract clauses, resulting in lost contextual relationships and misleading snippet retrieval. Graph-of-Thought holistic node traversal preserves document hierarchies, cross-references, and structural relations across all matter filings.
Unverified generative systems produce plausible-sounding hallucinated legal citations and phantom contract provisions. Strict 100% BELONGS_TO provenance audit: every synthesis bullet is bound to exact document, page number, and paragraph coordinates.
