GAS PIPELINE CODE: ASME B31.8 / AGA

“City gas distribution monitoring, custody flow regulation, and micro-leak gas balance algorithms eliminating unaccounted-for-gas (UFG).”
— THE OUTCOME-BASED REVOLUTION

TerraGridG manages city gas distribution (CGD) networks, City Gate Stations (CGS), and pressure reduction stations. It features real-time electronic volume correction (EVC), gas chromatography logging, automated odorant injection monitoring, and micro-leak gas balance models to eliminate Unaccounted-For-Gas (UFG) losses.
