Application Operational Details

What TerraBMS Will Do

Specific operational functions and engineering capabilities directly sourced from technical documentation.

⚡

Chiller System Manager (CSM)

Coordinates chillers, cooling towers, and pumps to operate at optimal thermodynamic performance coefficients.

⚡

Air Handling Unit (AHU) Manager

Regulates variable air volume (VAV) dampers, static duct pressure, and fresh air mix for occupant comfort and efficiency.

⚡

Approach Optimizer

Implements strategies to optimize the temperature approach in chillers and cooling towers, preventing energy penalties.

⚡

Intelligent Multi-Mode Scheduler

Automates modes of operation for energy efficiencies such as Free Cooling, Economizer, and Boost Cooling.

Real-World Case Study Outcomes

Operational Use Cases & Field Impact

Actual documented use cases across Quality Assurance (QA), Reduced Manpower, Efficiency, and Asset Productivity.

Efficiency

Efficiency (Primary & Secondary Circuit Optimization)

Enhances the efficiency of primary cooling circuits and optimizes secondary cooling circuits for maximum energy savings and thermal performance.

Productivity & Health

Productivity & Health (AI in HVAC Systems & IAQ)

BMS-intel integrates with HVAC systems to optimize temperature control, improve indoor air quality (IAQ), and lower maintenance costs.

Reduced Manpower

Reduced Manpower (Intelligent Multi-Mode Scheduling)

Intelligently schedules automated operational modes including Free Cooling, Economizer, and Boost Cooling based on ambient conditions.

Safety

Safety (Life-Safety & Fire Alarm Coordination)

Interfaces with addressable fire alarm panels to automatically isolate ventilation dampers, unlock egress paths, and recall elevators during emergencies.
