SUBSTATION BUS: IEC 61850 MMS
GOOSE TRIPPING: < 4 ms Wire-Speed

“Transforming transmission grid operations through automated COMTRADE harvest, waveform AI diagnostics, and nanosecond SOE timeline stitching.”
— THE OUTCOME-BASED REVOLUTION

TerraGridX is the disturbance intelligence platform for transmission utilities and substations. It automatically harvests COMTRADE oscillography files from distributed protection relays, uses AI waveform diagnostics (DFT, Fortescue sequence components, impedance loops) to locate fault distance down to tower numbers, and stitches nanosecond Sequence of Events (SOE).

What TerraGridX Will Do

Specific operational functions and engineering capabilities directly sourced from technical documentation.

⚡

Automated COMTRADE Harvest

Continuous retrieval of IEEE C37.111-1991/1999/2013 CFG, DAT, and CFF files across distributed IEDs.

⚡

Full-Cycle Sliding-Window DFT Phasor Engine

Extracts instantaneous fundamental phasors and symmetrical sequence components (positive, negative, zero).

⚡

K₀-Compensated R-X Impedance Loops

Computes fault impedance trajectory on complex R-X plane to pinpoint distance to line fault.

⚡

IEC 60255 IDMT Protection Curve Verification

Validates Standard Inverse, Very Inverse, and Extremely Inverse overcurrent trip curves against actual relay times.

Operational Use Cases & Field Impact

Productivity & SAIDI/SAIFI Reduction

Productivity & SAIDI/SAIFI Reduction (Fault Localization)

Calculates exact fault distance down to tower numbers within 30 seconds of line trip, guiding repair crews directly to the spot. (TerraGridX openwiki).

Reduced Manpower

Reduced Manpower (Automated Remote COMTRADE Ingestion)

Replaces manual substation visits with automated background harvesting of IEEE C37.111 COMTRADE files across all connected relays. (TerraGridX openwiki).

QA & Protection Coordination

QA & Protection Coordination (IEC 60255 IDMT Verification)

Compares recorded trip times against Standard Inverse, Very Inverse, and Extremely Inverse curves to flag miscoordinated relay settings. (TerraGridX openwiki).

Efficiency

Efficiency (Circuit Breaker Arcing & Timing Audit)

Measures trip coil currents and breaker arcing times during each fault to schedule breaker overhauls before failures occur.
