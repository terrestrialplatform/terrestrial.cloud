“Solar management should not be blind daily manual logging, but autonomous gigawatt-scale optimization delivering 96.5% plant availability and eliminating string-level downtime.”
— THE OUTCOME-BASED REVOLUTION

Deployed across massive 250MW utility footprints, TerraSolar unifies distributed solar infrastructure into a central Common Monitoring and Control Station (CMCS). Featuring LoRaWAN wireless tracking for String Monitoring Boxes (SMB), DLMS/COSEM energy meter integration, and real-time CUF/PR calculations, it drives generation efficiency and eliminates data blind spots.

Architectural Contrast

Comparison with Traditional SCADA / Legacy Systems

Point-by-point contrast demonstrating the technological shift from legacy industrial automation to the Terrestrial Platform.

Traditional Legacy Systems / Traditional SCADA TerraSolar Terrestrial Platform Advantage
Decentralized silos: five separate control rooms across blocks requiring redundant 24/7 technician shifts and manual paper log entries. Consolidated single Common Monitoring and Control Station (CMCS) with centralized domain expertise monitoring the entire 250MW footprint.
String-level blown fuses or dirt-induced dropouts go unnoticed for days until periodic manual testing is conducted. Continuous LoRaWAN wireless tracking down to discrete DC string currents with automated anomaly detection triggering immediate tickets.
Fragmented communication networks unable to bridge field inverters, DLMS meters, and high-voltage grid substations. Unified multi-protocol fabric combining OPC UA, Modbus, LoRaWAN, DLMS/COSEM, and IEC 61850 into ISA-101 compliant mimics.
