“Manage agreements and legal matters in one connected workspace—from contract obligations and key dates to case timelines, evidence, and source-backed answers.”
— TERRALC PLATFORM VISION

TerraLC combines contract lifecycle management and case management with litigation intelligence. Teams can organize agreements, track obligations and important dates, manage case records and timelines, and connect clauses, parties, events, and evidence for contextual review.

Platform Capabilities

Contract & Case Management, Connected by Legal Intelligence

Manage agreements across their lifecycle, coordinate case records and deadlines, and connect both to the evidence and context legal teams need.

Contract Lifecycle Management

Keep agreements organized from intake and review through execution and ongoing management. Track key dates, obligations, renewals, and related documents.

◉

Case Management

⚖

Strategy Q&A

◉

Court Hearing Mode

Use a focused hearing workflow to examine allegations, identify relevant exhibits, and retrieve page-and-paragraph evidence.

Interactive Knowledge Graph
