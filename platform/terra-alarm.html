TerraAlarm provides an enterprise-grade alarm management system built strictly to ANSI/ISA 18.2 and EEMUA 191 standards. Engineered with an enterprise event streaming core, it manages the complete alarm lifecycle, suppresses chattering alarms, and correlates Sequence of Events (SOE) across distributed substations and industrial plants.
