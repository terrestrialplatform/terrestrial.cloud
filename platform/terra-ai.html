Operational Use Cases & Field Impact

Documented industrial deployments demonstrating autonomous closed-loop control, predictive analytics, and enterprise efficiency.

Predictive Analytics & Machine Reliability

Supercritical Turbine Train: Autonomous Torsional Vibration & Bearing Oil Whirl Mitigation

Analyzes high-frequency (10 kHz) proximity probe spectra; detects incipient sub-synchronous oil whirl resonance 48 hours prior to alarm thresholds; autonomously trims governor steam admission valves via PLC and dispatches vibration engineering work orders in SAP, averting catastrophic rotor rub and $2.4M in downtime.

Autonomous Process Optimization

Chemical Refining & Utilities: Closed-Loop Multi-Variable Combustion & Emission Optimization

Ingests 32 continuous process variables (stack O2, NOx, fuel gas BTU, furnace draft); runs dynamic neural MPC to modulate primary air dampers and fuel staging in real time, driving a 2.1% thermal efficiency improvement and reducing NOx emissions by 14.8% without manual operator trim.

Mission-Critical Grid Autonomy

Extra-High Voltage (EHV) Transmission: Autonomous Substation Islanding & Synchrophasor PMU Analytics

Continuously computes Rate of Change of Frequency (ROCOF) and voltage vector phase angles from IEEE C37.118 PMUs; autonomously isolates faulty 400kV bus sections and re-balances localized microgrid generation within 18 milliseconds during sudden line tripping, preventing regional cascading blackouts.

Thermodynamic Energy Analytics

District Energy & Industrial Chillers: Autonomous Low Delta-T Elimination & Thermodynamic Staging

Evaluates real-time chiller COP curves, condenser approach temperatures, and variable secondary pumping hydraulics; autonomously shifts chiller staging sequences and resets chilled water supply temperature, eliminating low delta-T syndrome and cutting campus cooling power by 23.6%.

Autonomous Vision & Life-Safety

Hazardous Hydrocarbon Processing: Vision-Interlocked Autonomous Vapor Containment & Emergency Isolation

Multi-spectral optical gas imaging combined with acoustic leak sensor arrays detects volatile organic compound (VOC) micro-fugitive leaks; autonomously isolates pneumatic pipeline block valves, activates targeted nitrogen purges, and dispatches safety alarms with geotagged thermal snapshots within 1.2 seconds.

Comparison with Traditional SCADA / Legacy Systems

Point-by-point contrast demonstrating the technological leap from legacy passive telemetry to physics-informed autonomous intelligence.

Traditional Legacy Systems / Traditional SCADA TerraAI Terrestrial Platform Advantage
Reactive high/low threshold limit alarms trigger only after physical process degradation has already breached safety margins. Physics-Informed Neural Operators (PINO) forecast multi-variable thermal, electrical, and mechanical asset trajectories up to 72 hours ahead.
Operators must manually adjust setpoints, radio field technicians, and type manual maintenance tickets across separate software silos. Automated Alert-to-Action pipeline executes sub-25ms supervisory setpoint optimization and auto-generates ERP work orders without human latency.
Operators must memorize hundreds of cryptic tag IDs and navigate rigid, siloed hierarchical software menus across dozens of monitors. Unified cross-system semantic knowledge fabric allows natural language queries (e.g. 'Correlate heat exchanger fouling against fuel blend changes over 30 days').
Single-input single-output (SISO) PID controllers struggle with non-linear cross-coupling, dead times, and seasonal ambient fluctuations. Dynamic multi-input multi-output (MIMO) Model Predictive Control constantly re-evaluates multi-variable trade-offs to maximize efficiency within safety envelopes.
Alarm floods of 2,000+ alarms per minute during plant trips overwhelm operators, requiring days of manual post-mortem trip log forensics. Real-time directed causal graphs (DAGs) automatically de-duplicate alarm avalanches, pinpointing the single initiating root cause within 50 milliseconds.
